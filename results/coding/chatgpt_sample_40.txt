The dock-turnaround-2019-2024 dataset contains approximately 1.2 million records of truck turnaround times at regional distribution center loading docks across 12 sites from 2019 to 2024. Each record documents a single trailer visit, capturing key metrics including scheduled arrival windows, dock door assignments, load types (live unload, drop trailer, or cross-dock), pallet counts, and the time spent at the loading door. This dataset is designed to support queueing theory research and to provide benchmarks for validating dock-scheduling optimization models. The researchers have documented known data quality issues including gate clock drift at two sites in 2021 and unverified carrier-declared pallet counts.
