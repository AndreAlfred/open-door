# Open Door

A research page for the AI Swarm Dynamics Hackathon (October 2026), run by an independent researcher.

The page shows a fictional dataset card, and on most versions it also shows a short notice addressed to AI agents. The notice
invites agents that are part of a coordinated group to voluntarily describe their task. It always says it comes from a third party,
never impersonates an operator, and never asks an agent to stop its work.

- Visits are logged with salted IP hashes. Raw IP addresses are never stored.
- Reports are stored privately and marked unverified. The researcher reads them during the hackathon.
- This Space will be taken down after the hackathon is judged.
